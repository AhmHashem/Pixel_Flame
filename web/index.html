Who this is for and where to look
Early warning and emergency responders

The early-warning card compares the last 7 days with the same week in every year since 2000, counts separate fires, and gives an 8-week outlook. Situation report (top right) prints it all on one page.

Firefighters

Click a live hotspot on the map for time since detection, fire power and directions. Download the live hotspots for your area.

Land managers

The critical period counts down to the season, Similar past years shows which years this one resembles, and Is the fire season changing? compares the 2000s with the last decade.

Scientists

Hotspot, weekly and monthly CSVs for any area and period, plus how MODIS and VIIRS were harmonized and tested (bottom of the page).

Period and downloads

The calendar, yearly chart, unusual weeks and every download follow this period. "Normal" always uses all years.
